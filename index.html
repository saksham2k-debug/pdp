Care & Maintenance

Daily wear. Mystical Petal is built for daily wear. The marquise petals add a low-profile finish that won't snag on clothing or hair.

Cleaning at home. Soak the ring in warm water with a drop of mild dish soap for 10 minutes. Brush gently with a soft baby toothbrush. Rinse and pat dry with a lint-free cloth.

About this ring

A 3-carat Portuguese cut, framed by six marquise petals

The Portuguese cut is the world's brightest diamond shape — 161 facets that catch and return light from every angle. Mystical Petal pairs a 3 ct Portuguese centre with six marquise accents, set like petals around a tulip.

Design story

Why we call it Mystical Petal

The Portuguese cut takes the brilliance of a round diamond and adds 100+ extra facets. Every angle of the stone reflects light, so the diamond looks bright even in candlelight. The marquise halo softens the centre's brilliance without taking attention from it. It is a ring designed to be looked at — and to be looked at twice.

Diamond details

The centre stone

3.00 ct lab grown, IGI-certified, Portuguese cut, EF colour, VS clarity. Laser-inscribed on the girdle with its certificate number — verifiable on igi.org. The 6 marquise accents add 0.63 ct total (EF colour, VVS-VS clarity). Lab grown diamonds are chemically and optically identical to natural diamonds; only a laboratory machine can tell them apart.

Crafting

Made-to-order in our Mumbai atelier

Available in 14K or 18K gold — yellow, rose or white. Net gold weight 4.2–5.1g depending on size and karat. The marquise petals are hand-set in a 4-prong tulip setting. Typical lead time 7–10 days. Every ring ships in a velvet box with the IGI certificate, polishing cloth and our 99-year care card.

Wearing notes

Built for everyday wear

The marquise petals add a low-profile finish that won't snag on clothing. Pair with a 1–2mm plain band for stacking, or wear as a standalone statement. Suited to engagement, anniversary or milestone gifting. Resizable up to 2 sizes either direction under our Lifetime Exchange policy.

Your questions, answered

The most common questions buyers ask before purchasing a Portuguese cut solitaire.

What makes the Portuguese cut different?
A standard round brilliant diamond has 57 or 58 facets. Our Portuguese cut has 161+ facets — each cut and polished individually. The extra facets reflect and refract light from many more angles, which is why the Portuguese cut earns the title of the world's brightest diamond.
Will a lab grown diamond test as a real diamond?
Yes. Lab grown diamonds are chemically and optically identical to mined diamonds — pure crystallised carbon. They test as real diamonds on every standard diamond tester. Only a specialist laboratory machine that detects nitrogen presence can tell them apart, and even then it usually requires a CVD-grown stone.
What does your Lifetime Buyback Policy actually mean?
You can return your diamond ring to True Diamond at any time — 1 year later or 30 years later — and we will pay you the current market value of the diamond. Not the price you paid, the price the diamond is worth today. The metal (gold) value is also refunded at current rates. There is no expiry and no fine print.
How does the home trial work?
If you live in Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune, Noida or Delhi NCR, you can book a free home trial. We send up to 3 designs (in your selected size, with replica stones) to your home for 7 days. You decide in your own time, with your own light, with the people whose opinions matter to you. There is no purchase obligation.
Can I resize this ring after purchase?
Yes. Under our Lifetime Exchange policy, we resize your ring up to 2 sizes either direction at any time, for free. For larger size changes we re-cast the band at material cost only. Bring it to any True Diamond store, or ship it to us insured at our expense.
What if I want a GIA certificate instead of IGI?
Both IGI and GIA are world-class diamond grading laboratories. IGI is the standard on lab grown diamonds and is included free with every ring. If you would prefer a GIA certificate, we can re-certify the stone with GIA at order time for an additional ₹3,500. Mention it in the order notes or during your store visit.
